    Gestures are recognised entirely in your browser. For letters, only the motion of each finished letter is sent to the server.

    Pair an Apple Watch

    Adds wrist motion to letter recognition (gestures use the pen only). The watch sends its data through the SensorLog app on your iPhone.

    1. In SensorLog, choose to include the Apple Watch’s motion sensors.
    2. Set it to stream over HTTP, as JSON, to this address:
    3. Start streaming, then keep this page open while you write.
